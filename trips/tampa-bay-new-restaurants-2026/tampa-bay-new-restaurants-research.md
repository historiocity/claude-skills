## Excluded

- **The Landon** (South Howard, Tampa): Opened late March 2026, just outside your 6-month window. The Hell's Kitchen chef runs a street-food-leaning menu (birria dumplings, luxe wings), and reviewers say the room is very loud. A near-miss.
- **V Modern Italian** (Rome Collective, Tampa): Third US outpost of an international restaurant group (after Nashville and Charleston), which breaks your no-chains rule. It opened in September and has no meaningful reviews yet.
- **O-Ku** (Bayshore Gardens, Tampa): Polished Japanese, but it's a multi-city chain born in Charleston.
- **Forbici Modern Italian** (Sundial, St. Pete): Opened June 24. It's the group's second Forbici, reviewers repeatedly say live music makes it "very loud," and the menu sprawls (burgers, grain bowls, wings).
- **The Sapphire** (Westshore, Tampa): A 1950s supper club with VIP suites, live music and a late-night menu. Too scene-y for your taste.
- **Origen** (Dunedin): Opened in June. It's a Tulum-inspired Mexican and steak spot with a rooftop. The vibe-forward, tequila-led concept doesn't fit, though it's the only notable north-Pinellas opening.
- **Bosc Modern Bar + Kitchen** (Hyde Park, Tampa): Opened in February, outside the window. Sushi, steaks and pizza until 2am make it a generalist late-night spot.
- **Sundrop at The Luce** (St. Pete Beach): Opened in June inside a Marriott Tribute hotel. The coastal menu is competent but generic resort dining.
- **Bread & Vine** (Westchase Town Center): A French café-bistro open 9am–8pm with burgers and happy hour. Not a dinner destination.
- **31 South** (St. Petersburg Distillery): Opened May 1. It's a 45-seat speakeasy serving bites from Chef Johnathan Rodriguez, so it's a cocktail stop rather than a dinner. Good for a drink before or after.
- **Kojo (Central Park food hall)** (Downtown St. Pete): Pan-Asian, a cuisine you skip, and it opened in February.

## Watch list (not open yet)

- **Moon by Malio's** (Pass-a-Grille): Wild-caught-seafood fine dining from the Malio's owners. It was slated for September, but I couldn't confirm it has opened, and there are no reviews yet.
- **White Wolf + Carnivore Club** (Westshore Blvd, Tampa): Tastes Pretty Good (Rocca): a rustic Italian restaurant with a 28–34-seat wood-fired meat counter upstairs. Both are still "coming soon." This is the opening I'd watch most closely.
- **Kingfish** (Rome Collective, Tampa): Tastes Pretty Good's minimalist sushi bar, now pushed to Q1 2027.
- **Tokyo Swim Club** (Casa Gomez rooftop, Ybor): An izakaya and cocktail rooftop from the Kōsen team, due fall 2026.
- **Bararam** (St. Pete Beach): A small Spanish tapas and sherry bar from the owners of Sola Bistro, due this fall.
- **Toro** (Odessa (Gunn Hwy)): The Koya/Noble Rice team's casual sushi and ramen spot, due around October. It's the closest quality opening to Trinity and Westchase, but casual.
- **Peddler + Potter Oyster House** (Hyde Park (former Haven)): Bern's Steak House Company's seafood hearth, due fall 2027.
